INTERPRETATION
Logistic Regression outperformed Multinomial Naive Bayes on every metric: accuracy 89.7% vs 86.0% and F1 0.898 vs 0.859. Naive Bayes assumes words are independent, which is unrealistic for text, while Logistic Regression learns a weight for each term and copes better with correlated words.

Naive Bayes is slightly unbalanced: its precision for the positive class (0.873) is higher than its recall (0.845), so it misses more positive reviews than it wrongly labels positive and leans toward predicting negative. Logistic Regression is more balanced (positive precision 0.888, recall 0.909; negative precision 0.906, recall 0.884), and both classes get similar F1 (0.895 negative, 0.898 positive), so its errors are spread evenly.

LIMITATIONS
1. TF-IDF is a bag of words: word order and context are lost, so negation, sarcasm and mixed reviews are hard.
2. A single train/test split was used; cross-validation would give a more reliable estimate.
